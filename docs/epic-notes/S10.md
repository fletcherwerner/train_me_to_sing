# S10 — Note guide

## Built

- `src/core/feedback/noteGuide.ts`: `guideState`, `guideOffset`, `guideStateOffset`, `guideText` (pure, tested).
- `src/core/feedback/rescue.ts`: the Practise rescue threshold (1.0 s, or 60% of the note, minimum 0.5 s).
- `src/ui/components/NoteGuide.tsx` + `.css`: the guide (line, tolerance band, dot, ▲/▼, text).
- `src/ui/hooks/useGuideRescue.ts`: counts voiced, off-tolerance time per note at Practise You do.
- `Lesson.tsx`: guide at Learn (dot in We do and You do, line only in I do) and as the Practise rescue; `CentsMeter` removed from this screen; "The guide helped on N of M notes." (`guide-helped`) at Practise results.
- `MatchRun.tsx`: guide under the status and countdown; line only while the piano plays.
- `simSinger.ts`: `sharp300` and `findLate`.
- `RunRecord.guideHelped` (optional count), in the types, `buildRunRecord` and the zod schema.
- `tests/e2e/noteGuide.spec.ts`; `hardening.spec.ts` now measures the guide instead of `.cents-meter`.

## Deviations and assumptions

- The rescue adds up voiced off-tolerance time within a note (silence and being on the note do not reset it).
- Hysteresis is applied in the component (`wasOn`), which keeps `guideState` pure: `guideState(cents, tol, wasOn)`.
- The component's text updates only when the kind or direction changes (at most once a second), so the cents in the "near" text can lag the live value.
- The Learn guide is also shown when idle on a graded step, with "Sing to see your dot".
- The `CentsMeter` component stays for the Tuner and `AttemptDev`.

## Known issues

- `findLate` is added but not yet used by an e2e spec.
