# E08 — Stage 2 scales, tighter tolerances, latency calibration

## Built

- `src/content/builtin/stage2.ts`: eight lessons (s2-01..s2-08, `t2` then `t3`), registered in the same built-in source. No engine or Home change was needed for them to appear.
- Tolerance in plain words on the lesson intro (`src/ui/describeTolerance.ts`); Home shows a "stricter than Stage 1" note under the Stage 2 header.
- `src/core/scoring/onsets.ts` (`detectOnsets`) and `calibration.ts` (threshold, matching, median/round/clamp, `measureLatency`).
- `/settings/calibrate` (`src/ui/screens/Calibrate.tsx`): 4 count-in + 8 measured clicks at 60 bpm, shows "Measured delay: N ms" with Save / Retry.
- Settings: 0–500 ms slider (step 10) and a link to calibrate. Store: `setLatencyOffset(ms, calibrated)`.
- First Stage 2 lesson intro shows a one-time calibration hint until calibrated.
- Tests: `core/scoring/calibration.test.ts`, `audio/pitch/stage2Scoring.test.ts` (late300 + 0.3 s recorder offset all hit under `t4`; perfect s2-07 scores 1 under `t3`), content test for Stage 2, `tests/e2e/stage2.spec.ts`.

## Deviations / notes

- `settings.calibratedAt` (optional ISO string) added to the zod schema to know "never calibrated". Old data still loads. The slider does not set it.
- Verified: `Lesson.tsx` passes `latencyMs / 1000` to `recordFrames`, which subtracts it from frame times.
- The hint shows only on s2-01's intro ("first Stage 2 lesson").
- Calibration under `?sim=` uses a constant-rms synthetic source, so it reports "Couldn't hear clear sounds"; there is no simulated clap source. The measurement maths is unit tested instead.
- Threshold has a small floor (0.005 rms) so a perfectly silent count-in cannot give a zero threshold.
- The `Stage 1 lessons in order` content test now filters on stage 1.
- Goals are my wording.

## Known issues

- Tolerance fairness (step 6 of the review script) needs a human.
