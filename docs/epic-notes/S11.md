# S11 — Spike: hear the whole pattern, earn the solo, and a faster guide

## What was built

- **Whole-pattern call.** `playExercise` has `playCall`: the piano plays the exercise once at its tempo, then one beat of silence, then the usual 4-click lead-in. `PlaybackHandle` carries `callStartSec` and `callEndSec`; `startSec` is still the student's beat 0, so scoring is unchanged. `buildTimeline` (`core/exercise/timeline.ts`) holds the schedule.
- **Stage 1 You do** (lessons with levels and `stage === 1`, at Learn, Practise and Prove) uses `playCall` in place of `playStartNote`. Stage 2–3 keep the start note.
- **Bleed.** Frames before `callEndSec` are dropped by `recordFrames` (new `notBeforeSec` argument). The live "Sung" value is null during the call (`useLiveAttempt`).
- **Status line** (`run-status`): "Listen…" during the call, then "Your turn".
- **"Hear it again"** (`hear-again`): `playCallClip` plays the call only. No run, no attempt. Start is disabled while it plays.
- **We do gate.** "Next: You do" is primary only after an all-hit We do; otherwise "Sing along again" is primary and `wedo-gate` shows the line. `NEXT` is unchanged.
- **Practise unlock.** `learnPasses` (optional, zod and export merge) counts Stage 1 Learn passes. Two unlock Practise (`LEARN_PASSES_TO_UNLOCK` in `storage/level.ts`). An entry already at Practise or Prove keeps its level. Results say "Passed at Learn: 1 of 2. …" after the first.
- **Where Practise starts.** `createSession(levels, level, { guided })`; `BEGIN` at Practise, `ADVANCE` from Learn and `STEP_DOWN` to Practise land on We do when guided. Prove starts at You do.
- **Faster guide.** Visible text changes after the new state holds 35 ms (2 frames); the near cents number is live and rounded to 5. A hidden `aria-live` element (`note-guide-announce`) keeps the once-a-second rule. The dot's transition is 30 ms. The silence hold is still 250 ms. The held-cents state was removed, so the dot moves on the same render as the value.
- **Display smoothing.** `PITCH_CONFIG.SMOOTH_WINDOW_DISPLAY = 3`. `MicPitchSource` emits `midiFast` (3-frame) next to `midi` (5-frame); the guide, "Sung" label and piano use `midiFast ?? midi`. Scoring and recorded frames use `midi`.
- **Height.** At width ≥ 900 px the guide is `clamp(90px, 12vh, 130px)` and the pitch graph `clamp(100px, 13vh, 160px)`. Phone is unchanged.
- **Test singers.** `weDoMiss` (misses the last note in We do only) and `step300` (on the note, then +300 cents after 0.9 s; it stamps `window.__tmtsStepAt` for the response-time test). All singers already start at the student's beat 0.

## Tests

- Unit: session machine (guided flow), progress (`learnPasses`), `buildTimeline` (call), `smoothDisplay`, `NoteGuide`, `simSinger`.
- E2E: `tests/e2e/soloReadiness.spec.ts` (1280 and 393 px). Earlier specs that assumed one-pass unlock or a You do start at Practise were updated.

## Deviations and assumptions

- The Stage 2 spec line "one pass unlocks Practise" cannot apply: built-in Stage 2–3 lessons have `levels: false`, so they have no Practise. The e2e checks that they keep the start-note flow with no call, no "Hear it again" and no level message. The store keeps "one pass unlocks" for any non-Stage-1 level lesson (unit test).
- "Hear it again" is also shown in Stage 1 You do at Prove and in Today's practice reviews (the same screen).
- At Prove the piano shows no target keys while the call plays ("stays as today"); the sequence chips are hidden at Prove, but the status line ("Listen…" / "Your turn") still shows.
- Today's practice's Sol–mi step now ends its first Learn run with "Passed" (1 of 2), not "Level passed".
- The 2-frame hold is a 35 ms timer rather than a frame count, because `NoteGuide` re-renders only when the live value changes.

## Known issues

- The response-time test depends on the synthetic source's `requestAnimationFrame` cadence, not a real microphone.
