# E14 — Beginner path: new Stage 1, feedback levels, echo yourself

## Goal

Replace Stage 1 with a beginner path built on what research and established teaching say helps adults learn to sing in tune. The sources and how strong each finding is are in "Research basis" below. The points this epic acts on:

1. **Inaccurate singing is mostly a voice problem, not a hearing problem.** Most poor-pitch singers can tell pitches apart; they cannot yet turn a heard pitch into the right vocal action (Pfordresher & Brown 2007). So beginners need many short, sung matching attempts.
2. **Real-time visual feedback helps adult beginners**, most of all on short 4-note patterns (Berglin, Pfordresher & Demorest 2022, n = 84). **But continuous feedback can become a crutch:** singers do worse while watching it and retain more when it is reduced. So feedback is faded in three levels and a lesson is passed without it.
3. **Training across a wide pitch range may help** (Pfordresher & Greenspon 2025, partial support). So the key moves around the student's range.
4. **People match their own recorded voice better than anyone else's**, and the gain is largest for poor-pitch singers (Pfordresher & Mantell 2014). So the student can "echo yourself": match a recording of their own best take.
5. **Teaching order:** glides before fixed notes; sliding into a note is allowed while finding it, then the goal is to land on it; intervals introduced in the Kodály order (sol–mi, then la, then do–re–mi, do–mi–sol, then pentatonic and the 5-note scale).

E15 adds the daily practice session, two-day/two-key mastery and the review queue on top of this.

## What you will review

A new Stage 1 in four units: Find your voice, First intervals, Four-note patterns, Five-note scale. Each melodic lesson moves through three levels: **Learn** (live graph and piano, as today), **Practise** (piano only, graph after) and **Prove** (no live help, score after). A lesson is passed at Prove. Find your voice lessons hold single notes at different places in your range, with sliding allowed at first. After a good take you can echo yourself: the app plays your own voice and you match it. Old Stage 1 progress is gone; Stages 2 and 3 are unchanged.

## Specification

### C. Match lessons (`src/core/scoring/matchScoring.ts`, pure; screen in `Lesson.tsx` or a `MatchRun` component)

- Placement: `low` = range low + 2, `mid` = middle of the range (rounded down), `high` = range high − 2, each clamped inside the range. A fresh random choice within ±1 semitone of that point per attempt, so repeats are not identical.
- One target at a time: the piano plays the note for 1.5 s (I do), then the student sings. They have up to 8 s per target. The live graph shows a flat black target line and the red voice; the piano lights the target key.
- A target is **matched** when the voice stays within the tolerance's cents of the target for `holdSec` continuously. Reuse the note catcher's run logic or write a pure `matchTarget(frames, targetMidi, tol, holdSec, landWithinSec)` returning `{ matched, timeToMatchSec, reason? }`.
- `landWithinSec` (s1a-03): the time from the first voiced frame to the start of the matching hold must be ≤ 0.4 s, otherwise reason `slid` with the tip "You found it by sliding. Try to start right on the note."
- Score = matched / targets. Results list each target with matched / not and time to match.
- Match lessons have no levels (`levels: false`) and pass at 0.8 like today.

### D. Key per attempt (`src/core/exercise/fit.ts`)

- `fit(template, range, opts?)` gains `opts.tonicMidi`. Without it, behaviour is unchanged (centred).
- New `pickTonic(template, range, rng, avoid?)`: a random tonic such that every note is inside the range; if `avoid` is given and another tonic fits, it is not reused. Pure; `rng` injected for tests.
- Learn uses the centred key. Practise and Prove pick a new key each attempt (`avoid` = the previous attempt's tonic). The lesson screen shows the key's starting note ("Starting on F3").
- `?sim=` runs keep the centred key so existing tests stay stable unless a test passes `&key=random`.

### E. Feedback levels (`sessionMachine.ts`, `Lesson.tsx`)

For lessons with `levels: true`:

- **Learn**: today's I do → We do → You do, live graph and piano lighting the sung key. Passing You do at Learn unlocks Practise.
- **Practise**: You do only (I do and We do stay available via Back). The piano shows targets but not the sung key; the live graph shows targets only (no red line). The results screen shows the full graph. Passing unlocks Prove.
- **Prove**: You do only. No live graph, no sung key, no cents meter; the piano shows only the starting note before the count-in. Results as usual. **Passing at Prove passes the lesson.**
- A failed Practise or Prove attempt stays at that level. The student can step back down a level at any time ("Practise with help").
- The current level shows next to the step labels ("Level: Practise").
- Storage: progress gains `level: 'learn' | 'practise' | 'prove'` (the highest unlocked) and `passed` is set only by a Prove pass for these lessons. Lessons with `levels: false` keep today's rules.

### F. Echo yourself (`src/audio/takeRecorder.ts`, `Lesson.tsx`)

- While a We do or You do run records frames, also record the microphone audio of the run (`MediaRecorder` on the same `MediaStream`; on iOS Safari use the supported `audio/mp4`). Keep it in memory only, never in storage, and drop it on leaving the lesson.
- After a run, choose the **best take**: for Unit B, the whole run if every note was a hit and each note's median is within 25 cents; for match lessons (Unit A), the matched hold of the last matched target. If none qualifies, echo is not offered.
- "Echo yourself" button after a qualifying run (lessons with `echo: true`). It plays the best take (decoded to an `AudioBuffer`, trimmed to the notes' window) through the existing audio context in place of the piano, then runs a We do-style attempt against the same targets. It is scored and shown like We do and never saved.
- If `MediaRecorder` is missing or decoding fails, hide the button; nothing else changes.
- `sim=` modes: provide a synthetic take (a sine at the target pitch) so the flow can be tested.

### G. Removing old Stage 1 progress (`src/storage/`)

- Storage `version` stays 1 (the schema still validates; E15 moves to version 2).

## Review script

1. Reset progress. Home shows Stage 1 in four units, nothing passed, and the "Stage 1 has been rebuilt" notice if you had old progress.
2. Find one note: hear the note, slide into it, hold it. Each target says matched and how long it took.
3. Low, middle and high: the targets move around your range.
4. Land right on it: slide into a note on purpose; it says you slid. Start right on it; it matches.
5. Sol–mi: pass You do at Learn; the lesson moves to Practise. Run Practise: no red line while singing, full graph after. Pass; run Prove: no graph, no lit keys. Pass: Home shows Passed.
6. In Practise or Prove, note the starting note changes between attempts.
7. After a clean Unit B run, tap Echo yourself: you hear your own voice and match it.
8. Stage 2 and 3 lessons and imported songs behave as before.
